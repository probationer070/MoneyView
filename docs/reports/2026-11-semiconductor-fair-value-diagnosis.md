# Semiconductor fair value: which inputs drive the gap (2026-11-03)

Generated by `scripts/diagnose_fair_value_group.py "Semiconductor"` (and the same for the two peer industries below), on a temporary copy of the database; nothing in the app was edited and no valuation changed. Context: `docs/reports/2026-11-fair-value-whatif.md` (laggard margin rule rejected as the cause), plan `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-fair-value-refusal-outlier-plan.md` §10-§12. FV/P = Damodaran-style fair value per share / last stored close. Each sensitivity row changes ONE input and re-runs the engine on the stored case's payload; "engine refused" counts tickers for which the changed payload is rejected by the engine (not in the median).

Reading the inputs table: `g 1-5` is the model's clamped blend; `s2c ind/own` is sales to capital (industry used by the model / the company's own); `WACC0` is the company's WACC used for years 1-5, `WACC stable` the industry's cost of capital reached from year 6; `Term g` is riskfree (4.2%) except where the terminal return does not exceed the WACC (zero growth); `Implied g` is the year 1-5 growth that makes the case value equal the price (bisected in [-20%, 60%]; `above_range` = the price needs more than 60%). Bold = the named tickers.

## Findings (factual)

1. No single input explains the gap. The best single changes move the Semiconductor median from 0.19x to: margin target = industry margin 0.26x; WACC years 1-5 = industry 0.20x; growth cap removed 0.20x; every growth variant 0.19-0.20x. The terminal-ROC-only row reads 0.31x but only on 7 tickers (the engine refuses 17: the implied marginal return is below the industry ROC). A bundle of three generous changes (industry margin, flat industry WACC, 35% cap) gives 0.29x; no ticker reaches 1x in the bundle.
2. The market's assumption is far outside the model's: the price implies median year 1-5 growth of 39.4% (16 tickers; 8 more need more than 60%), against the model's median 4.7%. Semiconductor Equip implies 29.4% vs model 5.2%; Computers/Peripherals 32.9% vs 8.6%. Even for the highest-quality names (ADI, TXN, NXPI, QCOM, AVGO: margin 25-41%, terminal ROIC 27%) FV/P is 0.16-0.92x and the price implies 7-40% growth.
3. Growth does not move the group median but sets the top of the distribution. The blend averages the company's 3-year CAGR with the industry's 11.2%; 14 of 24 tickers have a CAGR of 0.1% or less (the 2022-25 downcycle: ADI, INTC, MCHP -27.8%, ON, STM, SWKS, TXN, NXPI, ...), so the model's g is 4.7% at the median. The 25% cap binds for only four tickers (ALAB, CRDO, MU, NVDA). Removing it moves NVDA 0.43x to 2.67x (uncapped own CAGR 120%: 20x) and nobody else; the median stays 0.20x.
4. Reinvestment, WACC and the terminal rule are not the cause. Industry sales to capital (1.21) is above the company's own for 22 of 24 (the model reinvests less than the companies do); switching to the company's own LOWERS the median to 0.14x. The company WACC in years 1-5 (median about 13%) is above the industry 10.6% stable WACC, but flattening to industry lifts only to 0.20x; using the company WACC as the stable one lowers to 0.17x. The terminal return exceeds the stable WACC for 23 of 24 tickers (only MXL takes the zero-growth fallback), so removing the zero-growth fallback changes nothing (one ticker is refused by the engine). Terminal value is 47-186% of enterprise value (median about 64%; MXL negative), terminal growth 4.2% at ROIC 27%.
5. Margin: leaders keep their margin and laggards move halfway to the industry's (median target about 24%). Setting every target to the company's own margin lowers the median (0.14x, 5 refused: the zero or negative margins), to the industry margin raises it to 0.26x. Base margins are depressed by the downcycle (group median 12.7%; INTC 0.0%, AMD 10.7%, MRVL -6.4%), so the value rests on a recovery the model grants only partially.
6. Dispersion: NVDA (0.43x base) and MU (0.31x) are the only mega-growth names; INTC (0.09x) and AMD (0.10x) are near-zero because margin is far below target. Under "all company-own" inputs INTC is engine-refused and AMD negative.
